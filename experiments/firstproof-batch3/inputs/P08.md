Let $M$ be a compact connected smooth manifold. Let $X$ be a smooth vector field on $M$. Assume that $X$ has the following property: If $f$ is a smooth function, and $u$ is a distribution, and we have $\mathcal{L}_X u = f$, then $u$ is a smooth function. Let $g_t$ denote the flow generated by $X$. Show that the flow $g_t$ has zero topological entropy.
