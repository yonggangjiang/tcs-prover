Let $H$ denote the hyperbolic plane lattice and let $E_8$ denote the negative definite $E_8$ root lattice. Let $\pi \colon \mathcal{X} \to \Delta$ be a Tyurin degeneration of K3 surfaces, with central fibre $X_0 = V_1 \cup V_2$, which is $H$-polarised in the following sense (this corrects \cite[Definition 3.20]{dfk3slpms} as it appears in the arXiv version, which omits the final condition in (2)): \begin{enumerate} \item (monodromy invariance) over the punctured disc $\Delta^*$ there is a trivial local subsystem $\mathcal{L}$ of $R^2\pi_*\mathbb{Z}$ with fibre $H$, so that for each $p \in \Delta^*$ the fibre $\mathcal{L}_p \subset H^2(X_p;\mathbb{Z})$ defines an $H$-quasi\-polarisation of $X_p$, \item (positivity) there exists an effective, flat, and nef Cartier divisor $A$ on $\mathcal{X}$ such that the restriction $A_p$ of $A$ to any fibre $X_p$ over $p \in \Delta^*$ is nef and big and satisfies $[A_p] \in \mathcal{L}_p$. Moreover, the restriction of $A$ to $V_i$ satisfies $(A|_{V_i})^2 > 0$ for each $i \in \{1,2\}$. \end{enumerate} Assume that this degeneration is very general, in the sense that the image of $0 \in \Delta$ under the map from $\Delta$ to the compactified moduli space of $H$-polarized K3 surfaces defined by Alexeev-Brunyate-Engel \cite{cmek3sspt} is not contained in any Noether-Lefschetz locus.
