\begin{document} \section{Problem statement} In the following, a monoid is an integral, saturated, sharp, commutative, unital semigroup.  Monoids are written additively.  A monoid $M$ induces a translation invariant partial order on its associated group $M^{\rm gp}$ such that $x \geq 0$ if and only if $x \in M$.  A valuative monoid is a monoid in which the associated partial order is a total order. If $M$ is a monoid, $x \in M^{\rm gp}$, and $y \in M$, we write $x \prec y$ to mean that there are integers $m$ and $n$ such that $my \leq x \leq ny$. A monoid homomorphism $f : M \to N$ is called \emph{infinitesimal} if the following condition holds: \begin{enumerate}[label=$\bullet$] \item If there are $x \in M^{\rm gp}$ and $y \in M$ such that $f(x) \in N$ and $x \prec y$ and $f(y) \prec f(x)$ then $x \in M$. \end{enumerate} If $P \xrightarrow f Q \xrightarrow g N$ are monoid homomorphisms, we say that $f$ is \emph{birhizomic} at $g$ if it is of finite presentation in the category of (integral, saturated, sharp) monoids and, for every factorization of $gf$ through monoid homomorphisms $P \xrightarrow h M \xrightarrow p N$ with $p$ infinitesimal, there is a unique monoid homomorphism $u : Q \to M$ with $pu = g$ and $uf = h$. A family of morphisms $P \rightarrow Q_i$ is called a birhizomic cover if, for every valuative monoid $V$ and every homomorphism $v : P \to V$, there is a factorization of $v$ through some $Q_i \to V$ such that $P \to Q_i$ is birhizomic at $Q_i \to V$. Let $P$ be a monoid.  Let $A_P$ be the category of monoid homomorphisms $P \to Q$.  The birhizomic covers form a Grothendieck topology on $A_P^{\rm op}$. Let $P$ be a monoid.  For any $x$ in $P$, there is an initial monoid homomorphism $P \to P[-x]^\sharp$ that sends $x$ to $0$.  Let $B_P$ be the full subcategory of $A_P$ consisting of homomorphisms $P \to P[-x]^\sharp$, for $x \in P$. We have given $A_P^{\rm op}$ the birhizomic topology and $B_P^{\rm op}$ the trivial topology in which all presheaves are sheaves.  The inclusion $B_P^{\rm op} \subset A_P^{\rm op}$ is left exact.  By left Kan extension and sheafification, it extends to a functor $\pi^{-1} : \operatorname{Sh}(B_P) \to \operatorname{Sh}(A_P)$ on the categories of sheaves.   Let $\pi_\ast$ be the right adjoint of $\pi^{-1}$. \paragraph{Question} \em Is it the case that, for all presheaves of abelian groups $F$ on $B_P^{\rm op}$, the morphism $$F \to \mathrm R \pi_\ast \pi^{-1} F$$ is a quasi-isomorphism? \end{document}
