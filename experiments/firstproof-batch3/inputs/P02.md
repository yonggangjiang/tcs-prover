\section{Problem Statement} Let $D\subseteq \R^d$, $d\ge 2$, denote the $d$-dimensional Euclidean unit ball. Given a function $f\in C^\alpha(D)$ with H"older-Zygmund smoothness $\alpha > 1$ on $D$ and let $u=u_f$ denote the unique solution of the following divergence form PDE (Darcy's problem) \[ \text{div}(f\nabla u)=1~~\text{on}~D,~~~u|_{\partial D} = 0.\] Consider the non-linear statistical inverse problem of reconstructing $f$ from noisy measurements of $u$ corrupted by Gaussian white noise \[Y_n= u_f + \frac 1{\sqrt n}\mathbb W.\] For $r\in (0,1)$ consider the following class of conductivities \[ f\in \mathcal F(\alpha, r) = \{ h\in C^\alpha(D),~\|h-\mathbf 1\|_{C^\alpha(D)} \le r \},\]  where $\mathbf 1$ denotes a  constant function with value $1$. For small (but fixed) $r\in(0,1)$, what is the minimax rate as $n\to\infty$ for estimating $f$ in the ${L^2}$-mean squared error  \[ \inf_{\hat f_N}\sup_{f_0\in \mathcal F(\alpha, r)} \E_{f_0}\big[ \|\hat f_n-f_0\|_{L^2(D)}^2\big],  \] up to logarithmic factors?
