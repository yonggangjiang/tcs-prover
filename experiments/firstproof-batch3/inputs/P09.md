Let $\M_g$ be the moduli space of genus $g$ Riemann surfaces.  We consider the Deligne-Mumford compactification $\overline\M_g = \M_g \sqcup \partial \M_g$.  The boundary $\partial \M_g$ admits a partition into strata  $\Delta_{G}$ parametrizing stable curves with dual graph $G$.   Let $V_0 \subset \M_g$ be a (complex) smooth subvariety of (complex) dimension $2$ that is totally geodesic with respect to the Teichm"uller metric.  Let $V_t$ be a holomorphic family of subvarieties of $\M_g$ deforming $V_0$, for $t$ in the unit disc $\Delta \subset \C$  \footnote{i.e. a complex submanifold $\mathcal V \subset \M_g \times \Delta$, with $V_t = \psi(\phi^{-1}(t))$, where $\phi: \mathcal V \to \Delta, \psi: \mathcal V \to \M_g$ are the natural projections; we will further assume that this gives a smoothly trivial family of submanifolds. }. We impose several technical assumptions on this family, namely (all closures below, e.g. $\overline V_t$, are taken in $\overline \M_g$): \begin{enumerate}[(i)] \item \label{item:smooth} for every boundary stratum $\Delta_{G}$, we have that $\overline V_t \cap \Delta_{G}$ is a (smoothly trivial) family, and \item \label{item:compatible} the families so produced are compatible with $V_0$ in the sense that if the boundary of $V_0$ in a smaller stratum arises from its boundary in a larger stratum, then the same holds for $V_t$.  That is, if $\Delta_G, \Delta_{G'}$ are strata with $\Delta_{G'}$ on the boundary of $\Delta_G$, and $U$ is an open set in $\overline \M_g$ with  \begin{align*} \overline{\overline V_0 \cap \Delta_{G}} \cap \Delta_{G'} \cap U  = \overline V_0 \cap \Delta_{G'} \cap U , \end{align*} then, for all $t$,\begin{align*}  \overline{\overline V_t \cap \Delta_{G}} \cap \Delta_{G'} \cap U= \overline V_t \cap \Delta_{G'} \cap U. \end{align*} \end{enumerate} Note that we do \emph{not} assume that $V_t$ is totally geodesic for $t\ne 0$.   Now fix $\Gamma$ a dual graph; we will assume for ease of notation that $\Delta_\Gamma$ is a product of lower complexity moduli spaces $\M_{g_1,n_1} \times \cdots \times \M_{g_k,n_k}$ \footnote{for some boundary strata, we would need to quotient this by a finite group; we exclude those here}.  For each $i$, let $\pi_i: \Delta_\Gamma \to \M_{g_i,n_i}$ be the natural projection map. Denote by $\partial V_t$ the closure of $V_t$ in $\overline{\M} _g$ intersected with $\Delta_\Gamma$.  Suppose that $\dim \partial V_t=1$ and that each $\partial V_t$ is constant on some factor $\M_{g_i,n_i}$ of $\Delta_\Gamma$, i.e. for some $i$, $\pi_i(\partial V_t)=\{Y_t\}$ for all $t$.   Prove that the family $\partial V_t$ is non-varying on that factor, i.e. $\pi_i(\partial V_t) = \{Y_0\}$ for all $t$.
