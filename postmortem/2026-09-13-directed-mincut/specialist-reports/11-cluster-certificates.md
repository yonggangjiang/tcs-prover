## 1. What the cluster tried and what it proved (summary)

The cluster is the implementation arm of A019's plan: build the arborescence packing by L023's load-threshold process, which needs, after each of O(m log N) monotone auxiliary-length increases, an in-arborescence whose length is at most 3/2 the minimum (`APPROACHES/A019-dynamic-threshold-trees.md:20-22, 38-46`). The chosen mechanism is to maintain Edmonds' laminar min-cost-arborescence dual y_S with slack: an arc is "eligible" if h_e = Σ_{S: e leaves S} y_S ≥ (2/3)ℓ_e, every supported set must induce a strongly connected eligible graph and every vertex must reach r in eligible arcs; then L017/L043 give a tree with one exit per supported set and cost ≤ (3/2)Σ y_S (A019:86; A035:14). What was proved, in order (SCRATCH/narrative.txt timestamps): L043 (18:40) an O(N)-size quotient-at-LCA representation of a frozen certificate and O(N) static tree reconstruction; L046 (A037, 18:43) local repair of one failed set by distance-ball downward dual transfer; L047 (A038, 18:49) complete deterministic repair after one length increase in O(nN log n); L048/L052 normalized-mass and total-variation potentials that "do not charge the work" (A038:221, 265); L049 (A039, 19:10) O((q+s+b) log n) local hierarchy updates plus an unimplemented boundary "Report(X,t)" oracle; L050 (A040, 19:12) static prefix rounding of dual labels; L051 (A041, 19:16) frozen-epoch omission debt with O(N) accepted scans per epoch; L053 (A042, 19:22) static O((n+f) log n) all-sets SCC tester; L055 (A044, 19:33–19:48) a barrier-descent first-failure connectivity tester conditional on an unverified dynamic min-ratio-cut oracle; L056 (A045, 19:48) a counterexample refuting the author's own restart-charging rule. Nothing in the cluster reduces the per-event repair bound below O(nN log n) or bounds the number of epochs; the author states this at every node (e.g. A038:183, A039:235, A041:261, A044:418, A045:200). ## 3. Driving assumptions, whether stated, whether tested

(D1) "The packing must be constructed by the L023 load process, hence a dynamic budget-meeting arborescence oracle is required." Stated explicitly (A019:20-22 "The local objective is either of two sufficient implementations of the budget-constrained load process"; A035:26 "The current reduction provides one part of that selection interface"). Tested only against its own sub-variants (L020, L027, the phase-count bound A019:196-266), never against the alternative of sampling a tree with good marginals without any packing. The author did later explore packing-free distributions (A022 entropy laws, A051–A060 undirected-tree entropy, narrative 20:58 "That could avoid maintaining directed trees") but always as an optimization over a parametrized law, never as a one-tree Markov update.

(D2) "A 3/2-approximate tree can be certified and maintained through Edmonds' laminar dual with slack 2/3, repaired after each length increase." Stated explicitly (A019:84-88 "A possible certificate with slack"; A035:14). Tested and found wanting within the cluster itself: monotone duals are impossible (A019:91-135, L020), one-event repair costs O(nN log n) (L047; A038:36 "This is a bound for one event"), and the natural amortization was refuted by the author's own L056. The factor-N gap identified at 18:49 (A038 written) never narrowed in the following hour (A039–A045).

(D3) Implicit and never stated: that the tree must be *certifiably* cheap in the worst case at every step, rather than cheap only in expectation/marginally. Because L007/L023 only need P[e∈T] ≤ c_e/K-type marginals, this assumption is what forces a deterministic certificate plus its dynamic maintenance. No node asks whether the certificate can be replaced by randomness (inferred from the absence of any such question in the cluster and from the 16 boilerplate-only occurrences of "random").

(D4) Implicit: per-event structural repair costs can be amortized over the O(m log N) events (A035:82 "A valid next result could either bound the total sizes of frozen epochs, or maintain the certificate ... with an independently charged bound"). Never established; A039:225 "There is no bound here asserting that the sum in brackets is O(N)"; A041:261 "The accepted-scan bound is per epoch. It does not bound the number ... of later epochs."

Audits. Batch 1 (before the cluster existed) already said "time-box A019" (`audit_history/2026-09-13T174157Z-audit-3-kimi-code-k3-6e3c6013.md:67`). The author's written response (index.md:81) accepted editorial fixes but not the judgment: "Their advice is not treated as a proof: ... a locally expensive implementation does not establish a lower bound on every maintenance strategy." ## 5. The single redirecting question

"L007/L023 only need the sampled tree's *marginals* to satisfy P[e∈T] ≲ c_e/λ (I2), not a worst-case cheap tree at every step — so why maintain any certificate at all? Starting from an arbitrary arborescence T, what is the cheapest randomized step producing T' with E[1_{T'} | T] ≤ (1_T + c/L)/2?" Asking this at 18:20 replaces the dynamic-oracle premise (D1–D3) with a one-tree marginal-contraction process (I4), and the natural way to realize the half-step — a fractional replacement F ≤ c/L with indegree one (I5), the observation that (1_T+F)/2 stays in the arborescence polytope (I6), unbiased rounding (I7) and Edmonds' two-branching decomposition (I8) — are all reachable from tools the agent already had (L019 existence, A028/L034's fundamental-path exchange graph, the exact-flow primitive in A001, and the dyadic-grid discipline of A040/A044).
