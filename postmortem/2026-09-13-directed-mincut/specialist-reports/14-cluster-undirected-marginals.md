## 3. Driving assumptions and whether they were stated/tested

**Assumption A ("distribution = static product law found by convex optimization").** Verified: A052's Background introduces μ_t at line 19 and every later node (A053–A060, L064–L073) optimizes or bounds the parameters t. A052 parents: A051 and **A022** (A052:2), the entropy-formulation lineage from segment 1 (A007→A022→A023–A027). The choice was made *before* L063 was even proved: at 21:01:20, while A051 was still being written by audit_cleanup, the root searched "radial isotropic position near linear algorithm leverage score prescribed scaling Hessian Laplacian" and opened arXiv 2504.05687 (Jambulapati–Li–Tian) (narrative.txt:8062–8067); at 21:04:19 it wrote "The remaining problem is constructing such a distribution fast enough. I'm checking its optimization structure against a recent implicit Newton method" (narrative.txt:8128–8129). The stated motivation for going *undirected* was Laplacian machinery, not sampling: A052 "It could use undirected Laplacian techniques even though individual tree samples are not directed" (A052 Connection section); A051 "could avoid maintaining or extracting directed arborescences" (A051 Connection). Never stated as an assumption; never tested. All tests (L065, L066, L069, L070) probe *implementations* of A, not A itself.

**Assumption B ("first compute a distribution/its marginals, then sample").** Verified: A055 §12: "access to one maximum-flow test after exact marginals are supplied is not access to those marginals" (A055:~470); A052 §5: the whole Hessian-oracle obstruction is about evaluating all marginals x_e = K_ee. The PDF sidesteps B completely: its "marginal" is 1_T of the current integral tree, which is free, and target marginals are only ever *bounded* by induction (Lemma 6.1), never computed. L063's own statement is for "any probability distribution" (PROVED.md:6199), so nothing in the proved mathematics forced B; it was an unexamined habit inherited from A022.

**Assumption C ("approximate degree balance is a numerical-precision problem").** Verified: L063 §4 counterexample and the deficit term D exist because the author expected d_v ≈ 1 only approximately (A051: "A proof using only this coordinatewise guarantee must pay for the accumulated error"). In the PDF every iterate is an actual arborescence, so d_v = 1 exactly at every step and the only approximation is the 2^{−t}1_{T_0} term. C is an artifact of A/B, not of the problem. Stated explicitly (L063 §4), tested (counterexample), but the conclusion drawn ("Directly maintaining a constant bound on D could be a different route", PROVED.md:6400) still presumes a fractional object.

## 5. What was missing after L063 to reach I4–I8, and why optimization was chosen

Missing, in order of dependence:
1. **The reframing** "I don't need to *know* the marginals of the sampling distribution; I only need to *bound* them, and a Markov-style process on single arborescences can guarantee bounds by induction." L063's hypothesis is exactly such a bound. The author instead read L063 as "find t such that μ_t's marginals satisfy the bound", which requires computing/certifying marginals (Assumption B).
2. **The transition itself (I4/I5).** The author already owned the exchange graph (L034) and knew its one defect (simultaneous exchanges may be invalid). The PDF's move is: put lower/upper bounds [1−q_e,1] on tree nodes and [0,q_f] on non-tree nodes, find a circulation, and *not* care that the result is not a valid simultaneous exchange.
3. **The half-step lemma (I6)** which turns "not a valid exchange" into "still in A_r after averaging with 1_T". The needed inequality x(E(S)) ≤ |S|−1 was already written by the author at PROVED.md:6306, but only to prove existence.
4. **Rounding + Edmonds decomposition (I7/I8)**, which return an actual tree so the next exchange graph exists. Both are textbook (Kang–Payor; Edmonds/Tarjan). Grep shows neither "Edmonds branching" nor any rounding technique was ever considered after line 8000 of the narrative.

Why optimization instead (verified evidence, interpretation marked): (a) Lineage: A052 descends from A022 (A052:2), and segment 1 had already established the "max-entropy product law" model (A022 conclusions: "obtained by minimizing the explicit finite convex function F"). (b) Literature pull: the very first action after conceiving A051 was a web search for a Laplacian/leverage-score Newton paper (narrative.txt:8062–8067), and the undirected relaxation was justified by "undirected Laplacian techniques" (A052). (c) Inference: the author's success metric was "prove a lemma per node"; the entropy route generated a steady stream of clean, auditable lemmas (10 in 65 min), which the harness rewards, whereas the transition idea would have required a design step with no lemma until I4–I8 were all in place. (d) A055 itself, the closest node, was created as a *fix for L066* ("avoiding an obstruction specific to exact mean balance", A055 Connection), i.e. as a repair inside the optimization program, not as a new sampler.

**Are "unused-capacity flows" (A055) or the "flow-relaxed dual" a disguised exchange-network circulation?** Partly, and instructively. Verified: A055 §1–2's certificate f ≥ 0, x + f ≤ u, d_v + div f(v) ≥ 1, tested by one maxflow with source arcs b_v⁺ and sink arcs b_v⁻ (A055:107–160), is a flow through residual capacity u − x that repairs out-degree deficits — the same *purpose* as the PDF's circulation (F ≤ q with degree 1), and its feasibility condition (7) b(S) ≤ a(δ⁺(S)) is a cut condition of the same flavour as PDF Lemma 2.2's Hall condition. Differences that make it *not* the PDF object: (i) A055's x is the marginal vector of a product law (unknowable without the whole machinery), whereas the PDF's is 1_T of one known tree; (ii) A055's f lives on the original graph, not on the arc-pair exchange network, so it has no fundamental-path structure and cannot say anything about tree-ness of x + f; the PDF's circulation is built on P_T(f) precisely so Lemma 3.1 can control x(E(S)) for connected T[S]; (iii) A055 uses f only to *certify* exposure of the existing law (eq. (4)–(5)), never to *move* the distribution. So: same primitive (maxflow with lower/upper bounds routing degree deficits through unused capacity), same intuition, applied to the wrong object (a fractional law instead of the current tree) and in the wrong role (certificate instead of update). The "flow-relaxed dual" (13)/(19) is the Lagrangian of that certificate and is even further from I5.

## 6. The single redirecting question

"L063 only needs the sampling distribution's marginals to be *bounded*, not *known*. Starting from one arborescence T (which has d_v = 1 exactly, so D = 0), can one maxflow on the L034 exchange graph with bounds [1−u_e,1] on tree arcs and [0,u_f] on non-tree arcs produce a random next arborescence T′ with E[1_{T′} | T] ≤ (1_T + u)/2 — so that after O(log n) rounds the marginals are ≤ u + 2^{−t}1_{T_0} without ever computing a marginal?"

Asking this at 21:02 would have (a) discarded Assumption B, (b) reused L034 as a flow network, (c) forced the author to confront "the circulation is not a valid exchange", whose resolution (average with 1_T, check A_r via x(E(S)) ≤ |S|−1 which it had at PROVED.md:6306) is I6, and (d) made "how do I get an integral tree back" the obvious next step, i.e. I7/I8.
