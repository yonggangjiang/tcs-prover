## 6. Top 3 lessons from this window

1. **The audits fixed the wrong target and the author obeyed.** Both Audit-2 and Audit-3 declared "an almost-linear construction and sampler for a feasible packing" the sole bottleneck and pointed to dynamic-arborescence/expander machinery; Audit-3 forbade reopening circulations. The author spent the window building components of that machine (L043, L046–L048) and a second recursion. A harness rule should force, at each audit resumption, an explicit "what is the minimal object that must be produced?" re-derivation: here L007+L063 only need a tree with E|T∩δ(S*)|<3/2, and L019 gives the packing's existence for free — the design question is a sampler with prescribed marginals, not a packing. Neither audits nor author ever asked "can the packing be used only existentially?".
2. **Verification and record hygiene consumed more than half of the window while the math was conditional.** Ten lemmas received 13 subagent audits, the root spent 41 heredoc writes on records, re-read the audit set four times and INITIAL_PROMPT five times, and all new lemmas are one-event, conditional, or obstruction results (A036/A037/A038 Conclusions; L048's own scope line). Require a "does this lemma change the main gap?" gate before promoting to PROVED.md, and keep audits and the prompt in a short summary file so compactions and turn boundaries cost seconds, not minutes.
3. **Ideas adjacent to the solution were routed to the wrong object.** Residual-graph reachability sets, persistent paired capacities, exact dual transfers preserving objective, and covariance-driven marginal correction were all used on cut regions or on a Gibbs law over all trees. The mechanism that was never tried is "take the current tree, compute a flow on tree/non-tree exchange arcs, round, split T+B by Edmonds, keep one half." A prompt-level heuristic worth adding: when a "maintain object X under updates" bound is stuck, try a Markov step on a single sample of X whose one-step conditional expectation is computable by one flow — the author had every ingredient (max-flow verified 18:01, L034 exchange compression, L019 existence) but no template for it.
