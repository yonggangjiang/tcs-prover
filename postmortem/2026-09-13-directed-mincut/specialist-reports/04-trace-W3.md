## 3. Near-misses against I4–I9

- **I5/I9 (exchange network, fundamental-path compression) — built, then pointed at the wrong problem.** A028 Detailed work (RUN/APPROACHES/A028-static-exchange-compression.md, "The explicit graph…"): "Make one auxiliary vertex for every original arc of G… For each e not in T and each f in T on the undirected T-path between e's endpoints, put an arc e->f of weight ell_e-ell_f. For every e not in T put an arc f_u->e of weight zero, where u is the tail of e and f_u is the unique outgoing T-arc at u." This is the PDF's exchange network (same-head rule mirrored to same-tail for in-arborescences), and its binary-lifting blocks (O(m+n log n)) play the role of the PDF's heavy-light/segment-tree compression (Lemma 5.1). The author used it to preserve *weighted walks and negative cycles* for a min-cost-tree repair oracle (L034 statement, PROVED.md:1118–1144) and disclaimed exactly the point the PDF's half-step lemma settles: L034 "does not assert that every negative cycle describes a valid simultaneous tree exchange, or provide an efficient update method when T changes." The PDF never applies exchanges; it averages the circulation with 1_T and lets Edmonds' decomposition produce the new tree. Verified: no occurrence of "circulation", "Edmonds", "polytope", "maximum flow" in A025–A032 or L030–L038 (grep, zero hits); "matroid intersection" framing at narrative:3219 and 3285.
- **I4 (single-tree iterative update with marginal contraction) — closest touch is A029/A030, under the wrong invariant.** A029 keeps h "particles" and couples them across activity halvings so that each remains an *exact* sample of the current product law (L035 statement PROVED.md:1145ff: "a coupling… uses the marginal p and a fresh sample conditioned on omitting that arc"). A030/L036 then proves every such coupling has expected recourse ≥ 2n/15 per halving (narrative:3815 [16:38:36]). The author treated per-step recourse as the bottleneck (3613 [16:35:48]); the PDF's update also rewrites the whole tree each step but needs only O(log n) steps, so per-step recourse is irrelevant. The load process (A019/A025/L031) is also one-tree-at-a-time, but the sequence of trees *is* the packing (MWU paradigm), so the tree must be chosen by a cost oracle — the dynamic oracle the author could not build.
- **I3 (existential use of Edmonds) — held but not used.** A024 Backgrounds: "The entropy formulation in L028 proves that some positive activities yield a feasible law… That theorem neither computes the minimizing activities nor provides a fast sampler." Existence is treated as motivation for construction, never as a feasibility certificate for a flow problem.

The only maxflow mention is a reasoning summary with no record: narrative:3756 [16:41:33] "Reviewing maxflow algorithm details"; also 4096 [16:58:22] "Exploring mincut approaches and local flows".

## 6. Top 3 lessons from this window

1. **Verification and record-keeping crowded out search.** Nine lemmas (802 lines) and 1,433 node lines were written in 49 minutes; 11 of 19 subagent results are audits of nodes minutes old; the root compaction fired while it was digesting a subagent's 292-line proof; a live prompt edit cost a full turn of reformatting. Local negative results (L036, L037, L038 = 298 lines) do not need three-agent audits. Budget: cap the length of CLOSED/obstruction proofs, audit only nodes on the critical path, and never route full subagent proofs through the root context.
2. **The author had the exchange network (A028) and the marginal target (L030) but bound them to the wrong invariant — constructing or maintaining the packing.** With the existence theorem already in hand (L019/L028), the productive question is "what is the cheapest random object whose marginals I can certify with one max-flow?" The harness should force, at each pivot, an explicit attempt to *use existence only as a feasibility certificate* (a flow/LP whose feasibility is implied by the existential packing) before building samplers or dynamic oracles.
3. **Obstruction lemmas were not translated into "what remains open".** L036 bounds per-step recourse, which only matters for polynomially many steps; L038 kills one static sampler; L037 kills one thinning rule. Each CLOSED node ended with the same sentence ("the original … remains unresolved") and the author moved to new machinery rather than asking which parameter regime the obstruction leaves untouched (e.g., O(log n) steps with Θ(m) recourse each is fine). Require every CLOSED node to state the quantitative scope of the obstruction and the nearest scheme it does not exclude.
