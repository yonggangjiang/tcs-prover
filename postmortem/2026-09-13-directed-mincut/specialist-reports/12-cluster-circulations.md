## 3. Conceptual gap between the author's circulations and the PDF's

Verified from the texts: the author's circulations are flows *in G* whose residual graph is read to locate or split cuts (A010:16 "choose or use a circulation so that the transformed graph exposes an unknown exact global minimum side"; A047:166 "f(S)=c_R(δ⁺_R(S))+d(S)"). The PDF's circulation is a flow in a *different* graph (exchange network, one in/out node pair per arc of G), whose throughput z is reinterpreted as a fractional tree-membership vector F (F_e=1−z_e on tree arcs, z_f on non-tree arcs; pdf.txt lines ~186–200). Consequences: (i) The author's circulation has to *succeed at finding the cut*, and L012/L021/L025 correctly prove that a circulation on c cannot do this without extra structure; the PDF's circulation never touches the cut — it only halves the gap between the current tree's indicator and the target marginals q, and the cut is found afterwards by L006, which the author already had. (ii) The author's progress measure is combinatorial per step (region vertex count ≤ 3h/4, A036:148; strict one-vertex decrease, A047:333–365) and every step must be *exact* (L045 (2), L058 (17)); the PDF's progress measure is an expectation that contracts geometrically (pdf Lemma 6.1) and individual steps are random and lossy. (iii) Edmonds' theorem plays opposite roles: the author treats packing existence (L019) as a target to *construct* (A008/A017/A019 outside cluster) or to *avoid* (this cluster); the PDF uses it twice purely as a feasibility certificate (Lemma 2.2, and the disjoint-branchings split of T+B). (iv) Lower bounds on arcs appear in the author's L013 proof and nowhere else; in the PDF they are the whole mechanism (tree arcs keep throughput in [1−q_e,1]).

Closest sentence (inference): PROVED.md:346 (L013 proof) — a circulation with prescribed lower bounds completed by one flow whose feasibility follows from a cut condition. It is the PDF's computational primitive for I5 applied to the wrong object (arcs leaving a known S instead of the arcs of the current tree). The sentence that most clearly shows why the gap was never crossed is A047:36: "Combining a capped batch with exact residual decomposition gives a direct alternative to constructing a packing of directed trees."
