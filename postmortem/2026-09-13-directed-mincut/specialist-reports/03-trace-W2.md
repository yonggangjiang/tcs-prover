## 6. Top 3 lessons from this window

1. **The author optimizes the wrong invariant: it keeps proving that its current construction cannot be sped up (L025, L027, L029 — three obstruction lemmas in 30 min) instead of asking what a sampler needs.** Each obstruction was aimed at the author's own sub-proposal (extraction-from-circulation, lazy retention, polynomial activity ratios) and each was answered by adding machinery (compact representations, entropy, Laplacian solvers). A harness rule such as "after any lower bound against your own route, write one paragraph stating the *minimal* object the downstream lemma (here L006+L007) actually consumes, and list at least one way to produce it without the obstructed construction" would have forced the sentence "L007 only needs one random tree with marginals ≤ α c_e/λ" to appear, at which point the acyclic product sampler (narrative 1677–1681) and the existential packing bound (A019:48) point straight at I4.

2. **Unrecorded 20-second pivots are where the solution was lost.** The polytope / union-of-arborescences / "sampler limitations" sequence (15:51:11–15:51:32) and the post-compaction widening (15:47:04–15:47:56) left zero trace in APPROACHES, so neither survived the next compaction (30 compactions in the run). The DAG records only ideas that were delegated and completed; ideas the root dismissed privately are invisible to audits and to the author's own future self. Require the root to log every dismissed direction as a one-line CLOSED/BLOCKED stub with the reason (here: why is a polytope-based sampler "limited"?), so audits can challenge the dismissal.

3. **Record maintenance was allowed to preempt research and to reset framing.** In this turn, ≥ 13 of 38 minutes went to writing/reformatting, including six minutes of retroactive opening rewrites for 23 nodes triggered by a live prompt edit, and the root's own definition of "done for this turn" (16:01:32) was a progress report. Every turn start and every subagent re-task begins with a full re-read of INITIAL_PROMPT.md (19 times in 39 minutes) and of the index, whose closing paragraph restates the same frontier ("implement the weighted threshold-cost tree process"), so each turn re-enters the same basin. Improvements: (i) freeze the prompt during a run and apply format changes in a separate cleanup pass; (ii) give the index a mandatory "what the downstream reduction minimally needs / what has NOT been tried" section separate from "active frontier", so recovery after compaction re-reads the open design space rather than the current dead end; (iii) limit consecutive obstruction lemmas on one route before requiring a route change.
