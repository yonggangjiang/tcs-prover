### C1. Defaults

**C1a. Standard service tier.** (pure efficiency)
(i) `DEFAULT_SPEED = "standard"` is in the tree. The leak: `~/.codex/config.toml:5 service_tier = "priority"`, and `speed_arguments("standard")` returns only `["--disable", "fast_mode"]` (pinned by `tests/test_context_economy.py:148`), so the standard branch inherits "priority" (transcript line 4 `serviceTier: "priority"`; in this run the CLI `-c service_tier="fast"` overrode it, so the leak affects only the new default). Remedies, in order: (a) remove `--ignore-user-config` from the app-server launch (C0.1); (b) pass an explicit `-c service_tier=<standard value>` on the standard branch once the value is established — the binary strings show `default_service_tier` but no enumerable values — and update the pinned test; (c) verify at runtime: the `thread/start` result carries `serviceTier`; emit a diagnostic and refuse to start when it differs from the requested one. `CODEX_HOME` isolation is an alternative but untested (auth lives there).
(ii) Saving 1 − 1/m for multiplier m: 33% if 1.5, 0 if the quota does not weight the tier. Unmeasured; F1 decides.
(iii) Same model, effort and tokens; only latency changes.

**C1b. Per-role effort.** (shared: effort on mathematics is a strength choice)
(i) Author and root critic stay at ultra (ROOT output is 484k of 1.83M; S9 puts the critic+writer tail at 2.8% of this bill at ultra, 1.5% at high). The landed SUBAGENT POLICY sets "verification, writing, and literature subagents with reasoning effort high" — this is too low for two of them: verification results corrected hypotheses in about half the passes (below), and writing-proofs subagents produced 683k output tokens of proofs. Recommend: LOOKUP and formatting at high/xhigh; VERIFY of a BEST-ROUTE lemma at ultra or xhigh; SOLVE and proof-writing at ultra. Set `agents.default_subagent_reasoning_effort` (landed as `subagent_effort`) accordingly. gpt-6-astra auditor ultra → xhigh is defensible (≈1–2% of quota per batch).
(ii) Verification output is 0.26–0.35M of 1.83M (hand/keyword classification); halving it saves ≈2–3% of the r=0.1 bill under the assumption that xhigh halves reasoning (unmeasured).

**C1c. Audit interval, time box.** (shared with 6.3) Keep `intervalHours: 2`, `timeoutMinutes` 25 → 20, three auditors, fresh-eyes solver on. Halving batches saves ≈1–2% quota and ≈35 min idle per avoided batch (one-sample estimates). The solver runs at ultra every batch and phased audits add calls: these S-costs are not netted anywhere in this document.

## E. Things that look like savings but are not, or that sacrifice strength

1. **Cutting author effort below ultra.** ROOT output is 484k of 1.83M; the author's reasoning is ≈5% of the bill. The failure was conceptual.
2. **Killing subagents.** The best early results came from open-ended subagents with interim messages; the fresh-eyes solver is the highest-value S-change. The cost was lifetime and inherited context (12.6 calls at ~120k per 1 KB result), not existence.
3. **Disabling web search.** Web is 2.1–2.5% static, ≈0 dynamic; exact primitives are needed. Cap and cache.
4. **Shrinking or lowering the critic.** The waste is the 900 s single-attempt timeout; fix with checkpointed parallel audits.
5. **Concurrency cap as a cost lever.** Same calls → same tokens.
6. **Read cap / audit digest / web diet on their own.** <1% under the sawtooth (S2 0.992, S4 0.999, S8 0.999); they pay only through C3/C4a. Do them; do not book them.
7. **Static attributions as savings.** Injected text 29.5%, rituals 8–11%, low-output calls 22.5% and the 41% messaging share are identities over the same tokens; double-booking them with C2a/C3/C4a is the error this revision removes.
8. **Dropping verification, or lowering verifiers to "high".** Verification is 19.7–21.9% of the bill and corrected hypotheses in about half its passes (C4b). Verify once per promoted lemma in a small context at ultra/xhigh; do not call it fruitless.
9. **Call caps as a mechanism.** Median task 10 calls, p90 24; caps of 30/40 bind one task in 125.
10. **Summaries instead of statements.** Postmortem C2/C5.
11. **Counting the tier switch as measured.** Docstring claim; F1 first.
12. **Reasoning replay off.** No Codex option; the replayed-reasoning share is unmeasured.
13. **A 120k ceiling without a cheap re-anchor.** Little or negative gain (0.881 before, 1.061 with the hidden-cost upper bound).
14. **Transcript delta suppression.** Disk only.
