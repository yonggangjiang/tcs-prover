## 1. What the cluster tried and what it proved

The cluster is the author's attempt to supply the one missing primitive of the conditional reduction (L006 + L007: a random in-arborescence with marginals ≤ c_e/P, P ≥ 2ρ/3, exposes a min cut with prob ≥ 1/2) by making the packing distribution a **product/exponential-family law** p_a(T) ∝ ∏_{e∈T} a_e over in-arborescences, with activities a_e chosen so that the marginals are feasible. A022/L028 (`RUN/PROVED.md:741`) proves existence via a finite convex program F(λ)=log Z(e^{−λ})+Σ u_e λ_e, identifies the minimiser as the unique max-entropy feasible law, and writes Z as a grounded-Laplacian determinant. A023/L029 (`:788`) refutes the author's own hope that polynomial activity ratios suffice (ratio ≥ (1/3)(3/2)^k on a unit-capacity graph with ρ=2; inverse entry 2^{k−2}; walk from a needs (2^k+3)/4 visits). A024/L030 (`:836`) gives a Sinkhorn-like coordinate procedure (halve a_e whenever p_e > 3c_e/(2K)) that terminates with feasible mass 2K/3 after Σ_{corrections} c_e ≤ 20K log|T_all| (O(m log m) halvings for unit capacities) — but requires an adaptive marginal oracle. A026/L032 (`:938`) is a polynomial-time O((n⁴+nm+B)polylog N) word-RAM sampler for a given product law (harmonic hitting probabilities, positive elimination, contraction). A027/L033 (`:1080`) converts dyadic activities into a unit-activity gadget graph of size O(N+Q) so that a uniform arborescence projects to the product law; each halving is an O(1)-size mutation. A029/L035 (`:1145`) bounds tree replacements when transporting an ensemble of samples through halvings (Σ p_t ≤ 30 log|T_all|, optimal coupling change prob p(1−p)/(2−p)) and proves three obstructions to the author's own ensemble implementation (Ω(hn²) copying, adaptive-selection bias 1/6, pool-only resampling loses support). A030/L036 (`:1340`) shows one legal halving forces expected symmetric difference exactly 2n/15 under every coupling. A031/L037 (`:1517`) shows independent thinning of light arcs destroys root reachability w.p. → 1. A032/L038 (`:1538`) shows the exponential-random-length min-arborescence sampler (and two variants) exposes the unique min cut w.p. ≤ 2(7/8)^t. A033/L039 (`:1638`) gives exact covariance-row update formulas, exact batching of same-arc halvings, and marginal-variation bounds 30(n−1)log|T_all| / 12(n−1)log|T_all| for halving/reset trajectories — still carrying a factor n. Net: existence, a polynomial pipeline (L030 + exact determinant marginals + L032), and six self-refutations; nothing almost-linear. The cluster's own final framing (`RUN/APPROACHES/index.md:75`): "Fast adaptive marginal decisions, weighted correction accounting, and a sufficiently fast final sampler" remain missing — i.e., it reduced the problem to a directed-determinantal marginal oracle that is harder than the original.

## 3. Driving assumptions, whether stated, whether tested

**A. The packing distribution must be explicitly represented by a parametric family (product/exponential family), and "sampling" means sampling from that fixed law i.i.d.** Never stated as an assumption; inherited from A007. A007's objective was "test whether the direct product-of-capacities law provides that event" (`A007-product-weighted-arborescences.md:~20`); when L009 killed it, A022 line 26 says: "simply setting activities equal to capacities gives probability at most exp(−Ω(√n)) ... That failure motivates optimizing the activities instead." The alternative fix — drop the product form — is nowhere considered: a grep of the 10 009-line narrative for "any distribution / non-product / without product / only the marginals" returns nothing. The reason product laws looked necessary is visible in A022 lines 100-126 and A026: they are the only tree family the author knew with (i) a compact parameter vector, (ii) determinant/Laplacian formulas for marginals (matrix-tree), (iii) known samplers (walk/Wilson, elimination). A022 line 30 states the objective as "prove that a finite parameter vector λ gives a feasible packing of the specified mass P, by minimizing the convex function F", i.e. the representation is assumed before the objective is posed. Tested? No — only its numerical consequences were tested (L029), and after each refutation the claim was narrowed rather than the assumption revisited (A022 line 137: "An exponential ratio between individual activities ... does not by itself force a superlinear total representation size"; root message ~16:10: "narrows the obstruction").

**B. Controlling marginals requires computing/detecting them (an adaptive marginal oracle).** Stated explicitly: A024 line 14 "Its marginal-detection subroutine must either return an arc with p_e>3c_e/(2K), or certify ..."; A024 line 18 (Objective): "an almost-linear implementation of this coordinate correction procedure, including marginal detection". Tested only in the sense that empirical estimation via retained samples was tried and shown biased (L035, A029 lines 169-185). Never asked whether marginals need to be *known* at all — the PDF never computes a marginal; feasibility of one flow (Lemma 2.2) is the only check.

**C. The object to control is the *law* (or its marginal vector) as a static state; progress = marginal variation.** Stated as the A033 target (A033 line 18 "bound or compactly maintain the actual changes of arc marginals ... K V_marg ≤ N^{1+o(1)}"), imported from audit-1 batch 1 (`audit_history/...T172233Z-audit-1-*.md:35-63`). This is the opposite orientation from I4, where the tree is the state and the marginal bound is a consequence of a contraction (p_{t+1} ≤ (p_t+q)/2), never maintained.

## 5. The one question that would have redirected the cluster

"L006/L007 consume only the *marginal vector* of whatever random tree the sampler outputs. Is there any step in the pipeline that needs the distribution to be *representable* (product law, activities, determinants) at all — or is it enough to (a) know from L019 that some x ∈ A_r with x ≤ c/λ exists, and (b) move a single current tree T to a random T′ whose conditional marginals are pulled halfway toward q = c/L, certified by one flow-feasibility check instead of a marginal oracle?"

Why this question in particular: at 15:51:28 the root was already thinking "basis polytopes and arborescences" and "matroid partition" (I6/I8), had L019 (I3), L006/L007 (I1/I2), circulations (A010/L012–L013), fundamental-path exchange graphs (L034, 16:34, I5/I9) and one-tree couplings across changing laws (L036 §6, I4-shaped). Asking what property of a product law is actually *used* downstream would have answered "none", and the remaining task — an update T→T′ with E[1_{T′}|T] ≤ (1_T+q)/2 — is exactly what circulations on the exchange graph, the polytope half-step, unbiased rounding, and Edmonds' two-arborescence split deliver (I4–I8). The audits pointed at the symptom ("replaces the oracle with something harder", `audit_history/...T174922Z-audit-2-*.md:39`) but not at this question.
