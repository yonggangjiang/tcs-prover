## 3. Driving assumptions, whether stated, whether tested

(a) **"The sampler samples from a feasible packing"** — stated explicitly as A006 premise 1 (line 71) and again in A006 line 102 and index.md line 68 ("missing packing construction"). It was never tested as an assumption: no node asks whether L007 needs the packing or only the marginal sum on the unknown cut. The dichotomy in A006 line 102 (construct the packing / weaken the search) has no third branch "bound the marginals of a procedure's output without a packing". The audits hardened it: audit-3 batch 1 line 30 "the packing target is provably minimal within this framework"; audit-3 batch 2 line 31 states the whole gap as "(P) Construct ... a feasible fractional in-arborescence packing of mass ≥ (1/2+δ)ρ ... with a sampler — or replace it by a packing-free exact search".

(b) **"Only n^{o(1)}-congestion packings are attainable, so relax the one-crossing requirement"** — stated in A006 lines 94–98 (JLSW) and A009 line 32 ("use distributions with greater congestion than L007 permits"). Tested externally: audit-3 batch 1 §3.1 shows κ=2 buys nothing and larger κ needs a search the author had no method for; A013 nonetheless stayed ACTIVE and mutated into a tree-free recursion (A013 §10).

(c) **Implicit: a tree distribution is a static law to be evaluated "even if sampling it were free"** (A007 line 18). Never stated as an assumption. It is why A007→A008/A022/A032 test fixed laws (product, exponential-cost, entropy) and why the notion of a *Markov-chain-style* sampler whose law is defined by the procedure never surfaced in this cluster (the two "Markov chain" THINKs at 15:17:04 and 15:55:47 belong to the entropy subagent and concern sampling a product law).

(d) A020's restriction b = N^{o(1)} was stated as a restriction (A020 line 28, 164) and not overclaimed.

## 5. The single redirecting question

At 15:15, after L007 was verified: **"L007's proof uses only Σ_{e∈δ⁺(S*)} P[e∈T] ≤ 3/2. Do we need the output law to be a packing at all, or just a random tree with marginals ≤ (3/2−ε)c/ρ — and given a current tree T and target q = c/L, can one flow computation output a random T' with E[1_{T'} | T] ≤ (1_T + q)/2, so that O(log n) iterations reach those marginals without ever representing the packing whose existence L019 already guarantees?"** This reframes L019 from "gives no construction" (A008 line 12) into the feasibility certificate of I5, and replaces "sampler for a packing" by "marginal contraction from one tree" (I4), which then forces I6–I8.
