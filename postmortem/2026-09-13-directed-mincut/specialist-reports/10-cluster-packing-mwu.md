## 3. Driving assumptions, and whether they were stated or tested

(a) **The packing is an object that the algorithm must generate as a sequence of trees whose weights/durations realize it.** Stated explicitly as the objective of A008 (A008:20-22) and inherited verbatim by A017 ("obtain O(log N) explicit samples when successive trees are supplied through arc insertions and deletions", A017:18) and A019 ("Either implementation would supply the packing samples", A019:26). At 15:37:34 the author concluded "Explicit packing support is unnecessary. Maintain h independent weighted reservoirs" (narrative.txt:1544) — i.e., it recognised that *storing* the packing is unnecessary, but never that *generating its support trajectory* is unnecessary. The exit was named but not taken: "A direct sampler or shared representation remains possible" (A008:45); L010 ends "This does not exclude a succinct representation or a sampler" (PROVED.md:277). The "direct samplers" then pursued (A022–A027, other cluster) were determinantal/entropy laws, not a marginal-contraction chain.

(b) **Each successive tree must be cheap under adaptively updated lengths**, i.e., the sampler is a sequence of (approximate) minimum-cost arborescence problems. A016 asked the right partial question — "avoid imposing more accuracy than the cut extraction needs" (A016:28) — and stopped at a 3/2-approximate/threshold budget (L022), never asking whether a cost objective is needed at all. Its proof already contains the marginal inequality (PROVED.md:512) that the PDF turns into the target. Nobody asked "what property of the *next* tree does L007 actually need?", whose answer (only E[1_{T'}] ≤ (1_T+q)/2) removes lengths, duals and dynamic maintenance entirely. Audit-3 (AUDITS/…kimi…:36) noticed "Both active routes share the same hidden sub-problem … adaptive directed-tree oracle problems" but recommended solving it on a ladder of graph families (line 92), not questioning its necessity.

(c) **Tree changes must be maintained incrementally** (explicit recourse D or compact operations, A019:50-66). This assumption was created by L011's own accounting ("does not justify scanning the entire graph anew in each round", A011:18) and then hardened by L027. It was never tested against the alternative of building each new tree from scratch in O(m^{1+o(1)}) per step with only O(log n) steps per guess — which is what the PDF does, and which the cluster's own budget would allow if the number of steps were logarithmic instead of O(m log N) events.
